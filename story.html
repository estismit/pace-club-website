Our story

Strength, made for women.

PACE Club began with a simple idea: women deserve a place to get strong that feels calm, personal and welcoming, without the intimidation of a big gym.

Why I started PACE

It started with the women I rehabilitated

Over the years, I've helped so many women recover from injuries that could have been prevented, if only they'd had the right training or the right programme.

Some of them weren't training at all, and their bodies were paying for it with aches and pains. Others had tried a conventional gym. Some were pushed too far, too soon, and got hurt. Some simply didn't know where or how to start. And for many, a big gym just felt too intimidating.

I've also heard so many stories of women who stopped training for years because they put their children and their families first. Now they're ready to start focusing on themselves again.

PACE Club is for those women. It's a community where you fall into small group classes that feel personal and individual, and where you can feel comfortable from the very first session. We adapt every exercise as needed, so whatever your fitness level, there's a place for you here.

Esti Smit, Founder

Why we're called PACE

Two meanings, one club

Where it began

Keep up with the pace of your life

Everyone's life moves at a different pace. A mom with little kids, big kids or no kids, a grandmother: each of us needs to be strong enough to keep up with our own circumstances. Getting strong at PACE Club helps you keep up with the pace of your life.

What it became

Train at your own pace

Then our ladies started saying "at your own pace". At first I wasn't sure, because I believe in pushing yourself a little. But I soon realised it's true: we do push, and the ladies push themselves, yet a woman who has trained for years doesn't move at the same pace as someone who has never trained before. At PACE Club, we accommodate everybody's pace.
