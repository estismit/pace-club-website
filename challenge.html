Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be fit to join?

No. The challenge is designed for women at every level, including those who haven't trained in years.

I have an old injury. Can I still take part?

Yes. Your coach asks about your injuries before you start and adapts every exercise to your body.

Where does the challenge take place?

At our studio in Winelands Medical Centre, 1D New Eskdale Street, Southern Paarl.

What is an InBody assessment?

A quick, non-invasive scan that measures your body composition, including muscle mass and body fat percentage. It helps us set your personalised goal.

How do I sign up?

Tap "Join the challenge". It opens WhatsApp, and we'll send you everything you need to get started.
