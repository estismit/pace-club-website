Why it's easier to keep going together

PACE Club · 3 min read

Most of us know how to start exercising. The hard part is still showing up in week six, when motivation has faded and life is busy.

Support makes a difference

Research on exercise habits keeps finding the same thing: people who feel supported by others are more likely to stick with regular exercise. Knowing that someone expects you, and will be glad to see you, is a powerful reason to get out of the car on a tired morning.

Why small groups work so well

  • You're seen. In a group of eight, your coach notices your form, your progress and the days you need an easier option.
  • You're known. Seeing the same faces each week turns training partners into friends.
  • You're encouraged. Celebrating each other's progress makes the hard sessions feel lighter.
  • You're never lost. There's no wandering around a busy gym wondering what to do next.

Community is part of wellness

Wellness isn't only about muscles and movement. Feeling connected and part of something matters too. Many of our members say the conversations and coffee after class are part of what keeps them coming back.

If you've struggled to stay consistent on your own, it may not be a motivation problem at all. You might just need your people.

This article is general information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition or injury, check with your healthcare provider before starting a new exercise programme.
